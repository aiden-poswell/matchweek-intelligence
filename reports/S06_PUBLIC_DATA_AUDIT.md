## Historical-only disclosure

Every probability shown here was computed retrospectively, using only results from before this match date. The modelling pipeline works at match-date granularity, not a validated precise kickoff timestamp with timezone, and it excludes every match on the target date itself from training. No forecast in this dataset was or could have been issued in advance of the match being played; this is a historical replay, not a live or prospective forecasting feed.

Both evaluated seasons (2024/25 and 2025/26) have already been inspected repeatedly during this project's own model development (S03 through S06). This is an exploratory, hindsight-informed historical replay, not a pristine, previously-unseen test.
